Services

Everything above the guttering.

Pitched and flat, new and old, domestic and commercial. If it is on the roof, it is what we do.

New roof installation

Full pitched and flat roof installation, roof lights, waterproofing and roof windows. Stripped back and rebuilt properly rather than covered over.

Roof repair & maintenance

Chimney flashing, felt repairs, lead valley repairs, slating and tiling. Most persistent leaks are leadwork rather than tiles, and that is where we look first.

Flat & fibreglass roofs

Flat roof replacement and GRP fibreglass systems for extensions, garages and dormers, with a proper falls design so water actually leaves the roof.

Guttering & skylights

Gutter cleaning, repair, replacement and downpipes, plus skylight installation and repair. Guarantee on all gutter work.
